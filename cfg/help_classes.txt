*** CHARACTER CLASSES ***

* needs Game Settings -> Gameplay FX
  -> New Specials (on by default).

SOLDIER
========================
Basic fighter, good all-around.
Weapon: Magical returning blade.
1. Charge: Run forward at high
   speed, damaging enemies.
2. Boomerang: Spiraling blade
   that hits multiple targets.
3. Whirl: Lash out at all nearby
   foes, may stun them.
4. Disarm: Temporarily disarm a
   nearby opponent.

BARBARIAN
========================
Tough fighter, resists magic.
Weapon: Heavy iron hammer.
1. Hurl Boulder: Throw rocks at
   enemies, damage by strength.
2. Exploding Boulder: Rock that
   explodes on impact.

ELF
========================
Small and hard to hit.
Has ForestWalk ability.
Weapon: Thrown stones.
1-4. Rocks: Increasing amounts
   of bouncing rocks.

ARCHER
========================
Fast with long-range arrows.
1. Fire Arrows: Spin and fire
   blazing arrows in all dirs.
2. Barrage: Stream of arrows at
   one target.
3. Exploding Bolt: Single arrow
   that explodes on impact.

MAGE
========================
Slow but powerful magic user.
Can teleport at any level.
1. Teleport: Random or memorized
   location (SHIFTER to mark).
2. Warp Space: Damages nearby
   foes with twisted space.
3. Time Stop: Freeze time for
   all non-team members.
   Rests 30s between casts*.
4. Energy Wave: Expanding flood
   of energy through walls.
5. HeartBurst: Enemies burst into
   flames from within!

ARCHMAGE
========================
Specialized Mage (level 6+).
Extreme firepower, very weak.
1. Teleport: Same as Mage.
2. HeartBurst / Chain Lightning
   (SHIFTER for chain).
3. Summon Image / Fire Elemental
   (SHIFTER for elemental).
4. Mind Control: Bend enemies
   to your will temporarily.
   Rests 20s between casts*.

CLERIC
========================
Slow healer, generates barriers.
1. Heal: Heal nearby friends.
   SHIFTER: Mystic Mace defense.
2. Raise Undead: Bring back the
   fallen. SHIFTER: Turn Undead.
3. Raise Ghost: Summon spirits.
   SHIFTER: Lay spirits to rest.
4. Resurrect: Full resurrection
   of fallen comrades.

THIEF
========================
Fast with unlimited daggers.
1. Plant Bomb: Drop explosives.
   SHIFTER: Mine*, unseen until
   a foe steps on it.
2. Cloak: Become invisible.
3. Taunt: Lure enemies away.
   SHIFTER: Charm enemies.
4. Poison Cloud: Create noxious
   cloud to damage enemies.

DRUID
========================
Nature magician, throws lightning.
1. Grow Tree: Plant trees that
   block enemies but not friends.
2. Summon: Call sprites that
   freeze foes.
3. Reveal Items: Show treasure
   and generators on radar.
4. Protection: Wind shield for
   nearby teammates.

GHOST
========================
Passes through walls.
1. Scare: Make enemies flee.
   SHIFTER: Wail*, fear that
   leaps from foe to foe.
   Both rest 8s between casts*.
2. Siphon*: A touch that heals
   you by half the harm it does.
3. Possess*: Enter a foe and
   drive it for a while.
4. Phase*: Untouchable and fast
   for a few seconds, no attacks.

FAERIE
========================
Flying, freezing powder.
1. Blink*: Short random hop.
   SHIFTER: Swap*, trade places
   with anyone in sight.
2. Glimmer*: A brief freeze in
   all eight directions; rests
   15s between casts.
3. Hasten*: Speed up an ally.
   SHIFTER: Haste Self*, speed
   yourself up.
4. Wish*: The faerie dies; a
   fallen ally rises and allies
   nearby are healed.

SKELETON
========================
Fast, can Tunnel.
1. Tunnel: Burrow to a spot
   nearby.
2. Dig In*: Sink into the floor,
   unseen and mending.
3. Bone Wall*: Line of bones that
   blocks the way for a while.
   SHIFTER: Bone Storm*, shatter
   all your walls.
4. Reassemble*: Ward your next
   death, rise at quarter health.
   SHIFTER: Legion*, half your
   kills rise as your skeletons.

FIRE ELEMENTAL
========================
Fast; bursts into flame on death.
1. Starburst: Fling flaming
   meteors in all directions.
2. Immolate*: Burn all who touch
   you, leave burning footprints.
3. Meteor Rain*: Meteors fall on
   a distant area.
4. Rekindle*: Burn magic into
   health. SHIFTER: Supernova*,
   die in one huge blast.

ORC CAPTAIN
========================
Promoted Orc (level 5+).
1. Howl*: Stun nearby enemies;
   rests 10s between casts.
2. Eat Corpse*: Heal by eating
   the fallen.
3. Hook Blade*: Spiraling knife
   reels a foe to your feet.
   SHIFTER: Knife Fan*, three
   knives in a spread.
4. War Banner*: Plant it on a
   corpse; allies near it mend
   and never flee.
   SHIFTER: Warband*, grunts
   charge in to help.

OTHER CLASSES
========================
Orc: Strong melee, Howl stuns
   (rests 10s between casts*).
Slime: Grows and splits in two.
