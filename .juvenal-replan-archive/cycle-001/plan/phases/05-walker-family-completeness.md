## Implementation Details
- Existing `family_*_scen99` rows (one per family) are kept. This phase audits each row for at least one RNG-insensitive predicate from the set: `WalkerFamilyCount`, `EffectFamilyCount`, `WeaponFamilyEmitted`, `EventKindAtLeast`/`EventKindExactly`, `WalkerDiedByFinal`, `WalkerAliveAtFinal`, `TreasureFamilyOfOrderRemovedFromOblist`, `LevelDoneEquals`, `TickReached`. Rows lacking one get an additional predicate, not a widening of existing ones.
- Walker families with no row at all (none currently per the manifest, but verifier still asserts presence) get a brand new row, registered with `OG_PARITY_TEST(...)`.
- Mirror `scenario_table.h` to companion; rebuild companion binary; recapture goldens via `scripts/parity/capture_master_golden.sh --all` (or per-row).
